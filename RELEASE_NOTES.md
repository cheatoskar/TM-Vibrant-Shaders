## TM Vibrant Shaders 1.0.0 Beta 1

Real-time shaders for TrackMania Nations and United Forever, in the style of Minecraft shader packs like Sildur's Vibrant, BSL and IterationT.

> **Beta:** the presets are still being tuned, so the look will change with the next versions. Feedback and screenshots (`F12` captures) are welcome in [Issues](https://github.com/cheatoskar/tmnf-vibrant-shaders/issues).

### Download

**`TM-Vibrant-Shaders.zip`** is all you need. Unzip it and run **`TM-Vibrant-Shaders-Setup.exe`**. You can install:
- **for the [TrackMania ModLoader](https://tomashu.dev/software/tmloader/)**, then tick the mod in the ModLoader, or
- **into the game folder without the ModLoader**: the setup adds a `d3d9.dll` next to `TmForever.exe`.

`README.txt` in the zip explains both ways, and the manual install without the setup, in English and German.

### What it does

- **Lighting:** ambient occlusion, sun shadows, sun- and sky-coloured light, light shafts and height fog.
- **Image:** HDR glow for neon borders and floodlights, bloom, auto exposure, filmic tone mapping, FXAA and sharpening.
- **Custom skies:** clear sky with clouds, starry night, a ray-traced **black hole with a ringed planet**, and aurora.
- **Presets:** 10 built-in presets, your own named presets, and an automatic preset per map mood (day / sunset / night).
- **In-game menu:** `F8` opens the menu, `F7` toggles the shaders, and every change is saved automatically.
- **Under the HUD:** effects are drawn before the HUD, so the interface stays sharp. They also apply in replays and video export.
